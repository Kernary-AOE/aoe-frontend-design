# MANIFEST — what comes from where

This file documents what's in this repo and where each artifact
originated in the source monorepo (`prime` — the private development
monorepo). It exists for fork-clarity; if you're consuming the corpus,
you don't need to read this.

moving them into a corpus repo would be miscategorisation.

## Brand-name persona scope

`primes-v3/sources/@community/persona-*.prime` includes 21 brand-name
personas (Apple, Linear, Stripe, Vercel, Notion, Spotify, Figma,
Framer, Airbnb, Coinbase, Toss, Warp, Posthog, Mintlify, Raycast,
Sanity, Sentry, Replicate, Superhuman, Supabase, Intercom). Each is a
**descriptive atom** capturing observable, public design characteristics
(font choices, color palette, density, motion language) — they are
**reference atoms**, not redistributions of any proprietary asset, code,
or trademark. The relevant trademark notice is in `NOTICE`.

If your fork wants to drop these for legal cleanliness, delete the
files in `@community/persona-<brand>.*` and re-run `prime check
--registry` — the corpus will recover with reduced register variety
(11 personas remain in `@impeccable/`).
