# CherryPickingEvidence [anti-pattern] v1.0.0
Selecting only the research data points that support a predetermined design direction while ignoring contradictory signals — creating false confidence in a chosen solution while real problems go unaddressed.
domain: frontend-design

## Label
Cherry-Picking Evidence

## Trap
Design direction was already decided by stakeholders; the desire to appear evidence-based without actually changing direction leads to selectively citing research. Trust in the research practice erodes when the same problems resurface.

## Remediation
Actively look for contradictory signals in all available data before finalizing a recommendation. If research conflicts with the chosen direction, surface the conflict explicitly rather than omitting it.
