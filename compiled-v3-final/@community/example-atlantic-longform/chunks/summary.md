# AtlanticLongform [example] v1.0.0
The Atlantic's longform article opens with a full-bleed cover image (16:9, ~80vh tall) overlaid with a 64px serif headline (their custom 'Atlantic Serif'), 24px deck below in italic, and byline. The body uses a 680px max-width centered column, 19px/32px serif body, with first paragraph styled with a 5em drop-cap. Pull-quotes are 28px italic, indented 80px, with thin 1px rules above/below.
domain: typography
