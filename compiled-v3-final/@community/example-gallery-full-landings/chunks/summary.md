# FullLandingGallery [example] v1.0.0
20 curated full-page landing screenshots from Land-book Pro, tagged by pattern type and persona lean. Full-page screenshots capture what section-level galleries cannot: page rhythm, section sequencing, visual consistency, and scroll narrative. A hero section and a pricing section may each look good in isolation — but a full-page view reveals whether transitions are smooth, whether visual density varies appropriately across the scroll, and whether the brand aesthetic holds from top to bottom.
