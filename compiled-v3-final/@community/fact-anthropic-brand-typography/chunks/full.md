# AnthropicBrandTypography [fact] v1.0.0
Anthropic's official brand typography uses Poppins (Arial fallback) for headings at 24pt and above, and Lora (Georgia fallback) for all body text — making it an intentional serif/sans-serif split that grounds brand communication in warmth and authority.
> Anthropic brand design mandates Poppins (weight 600–700) for all heading levels at 24pt+ with Arial as system fallback, and Lora (weight 400) for body text at 16–18px with Georgia as system fallback; the pairing communicates intellectual credibility via the serif body against contemporary geometric display headings.
domain: frontend-design

## Confidence
strong

## Applies To
- any artifact built for or on behalf of Anthropic
- claude.ai interface components
- Anthropic marketing pages and documentation

## Quantitative
- **Heading Font**: Poppins
- **Heading Fallback**: Arial, sans-serif
- **Heading Min Size**: 24pt
- **Heading Weight**: 600–700
- **Body Font**: Lora
- **Body Fallback**: Georgia, serif
- **Body Size**: 16–18px
- **Body Weight**: 400

## Counter Conditions
- Third-party integrations referencing Anthropic API may use their own brand typography.
- Documentation sites (docs.anthropic.com) may shift to a more neutral technical font for code-heavy content.
