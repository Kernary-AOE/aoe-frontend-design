# OpportunityCostOfDefaults [fact] v1.0.0
Every design default accepted without questioning has a hidden opportunity cost: the distinctive visual identity that a deliberate choice would have created. Accepting Tailwind's violet-500, Inter, and rounded-2xl simultaneously forfeits three independent opportunities to differentiate.
> Each of the five AI-slop signal categories (color, typography, radius, shadow, layout) is an independent differentiation opportunity. Accepting the default in any one category reduces the design's distinctiveness by roughly 20%. Accepting all five defaults produces output indistinguishable from 70%+ of AI-generated work — which is the statistical outcome of five compounded non-decisions.
