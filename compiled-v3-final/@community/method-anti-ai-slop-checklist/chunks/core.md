# AntiAiSlopChecklist [method] v1.0.0
A structured pre-ship audit of the five AI fingerprint categories — color, typography, layout, visual effects, motion — that distinguishes intentionally designed output from statistical-average AI output.
domain: frontend-design

## Label
Anti-AI-Slop Comprehensive Checklist

## Trigger
Before shipping any AI-generated or AI-assisted frontend. The test: show the interface to someone and say 'AI made this.' If they believe you immediately — redesign it.

## Pass Condition
0 failed checks = distinctive. 1-3 = targeted fixes. 4-6 = generic AI output. 7+ = restart from persona selection.
