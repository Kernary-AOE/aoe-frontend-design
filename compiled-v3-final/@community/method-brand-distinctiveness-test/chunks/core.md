# BrandDistinctivenessTest [method] v1.0.0
A 5-question audit that tests whether a design communicates specific brand identity rather than generic AI-generated output — applied before any public launch or design review.
domain: frontend-design

## Label
Brand Distinctiveness Test

## Scoring
5/5 = distinctive brand identity. 3-4/5 = targeted improvements needed. 0-2/5 = generic output, restart from persona brief.
