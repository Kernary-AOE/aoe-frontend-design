# DesignQaPreShip [method] v1.0.0
A structured checklist for reviewing UX flows, design consistency, accessibility, and content quality before releasing any user-facing feature — covering the 10 categories most commonly skipped in AI-generated outputs.
domain: frontend-design

## Label
Design QA Pre-Ship Checklist

## Pass Condition
All items pass before the feature is marked done.

## Items
- Users can complete key tasks without confusion
- Multi-step flows are labeled, ordered, and provide per-step feedback
- Destructive or irreversible actions are visually differentiated and confirmed
- Layout follows project spacing, typography, and visual language tokens
- Navigation labels, headings, and actions are consistent across screens
- Keyboard-only flow completion works and focus returns to sensible locations
- Headings, landmarks, and labels are present and meaningful for screen readers
- Text and interactive elements meet WCAG AA contrast (@community/constraint-accessibility-aa-minimum)
- Error and success messages are actionable and context-specific
- Copy is clear, concise, and consistent with the voice-and-tone guide
