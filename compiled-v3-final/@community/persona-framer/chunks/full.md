# Framer [persona] v1.0.0
Dark-first cinematic tool aesthetic: GT Walsheim at 500 weight only with extreme -5.5px tracking at 110px making headlines feel spring-loaded, void-black #000000 canvas with electric Framer Blue #0099ff as sole accent, and Inter Variable with 6+ OpenType features (cv01, cv05, cv09, cv11, ss03, ss07) giving body text a subtly custom character.
domain: visual-design

## School
framer

## Implies
- **Font**:
  - **Display**: GT Walsheim Framer Medium / GT Walsheim Medium — weight 500 only, never bold (700), never regular (400); -5.5px tracking at 110px / -4.25px at 85px / -3.1px at 62px
  - **Body**: Inter Variable with OpenType cv01, cv05, cv09, cv11, ss03, ss07 — 15px nav/UI; Mona Sans weight 100 for ultra-light ethereal accents
  - **Monospace**: Azeret Mono — also Open Runde as rounded micro-companion for tags/badges
- **Color**:
  - **Temperature**: cool
  - **Palette**: void black #000000 absolute (not #0a0a0a), pure white #ffffff text, Framer Blue #0099ff sole accent, blue glow ring rgba(0,153,255,0.15) borders, muted silver #a6a6a6 secondary, elevated dark surface #090909, frosted glass rgba(255,255,255,0.1) buttons
  - **Background**: #000000 pure void black — not warm charcoal, not near-black, void is sacred
- **Density**: cinematic spacious — 110px display with line-height 0.85 (sub-baseline stacking), section padding 80–120px vertical, max content ~1200px, 100px full-pill CTAs
- **Layout**: full-bleed dark canvas, product screenshots as hero art, feature sections with blue-ring-bordered cards, pill-only CTAs (40–100px radius throughout)
- **Imagery**: full-fidelity product screenshots dominate as hero art — Framer as proof-of-concept; no stock photography; the tool demonstrates itself
- **Motion**: kinetic — GT Walsheim's extreme tracking makes headlines feel spring-loaded even statically; animated product demos, elevated float shadow (rgba(255,255,255,0.1) top highlight + rgba(0,0,0,0.25) deep ambient), spring-config for interactive elements
