# Intercom [persona] v1.0.0
Warm editorial B2B platform: Saans on cream-white #faf9f6 canvas, Fin Orange #ff5600 as singular AI accent, and near-rectangular 4px button radius that creates industrial precision against cozy warm neutrals.
domain: visual-design

## School
intercom

## Implies
- **Font**:
  - **Display**: Saans (fallbacks: Saans Fallback, ui-sans-serif, system-ui) — 80px/400/line-height 1.00/letter-spacing -2.4px for hero
  - **Body**: Serrif serif (fallbacks: Serrif Fallback, ui-serif, Georgia) — 16px/300/line-height 1.40/letter-spacing -0.16px
  - **Monospace**: SaansMono (fallbacks: SaansMono Fallback, ui-monospace) — 12px uppercase with positive tracking 0.6–1.2px
  - **Accent**: MediumLL / LLMedium (fallbacks: system-ui, -apple-system) — UI chrome and emphasis
- **Color**:
  - **Temperature**: warm-neutral
  - **Palette**: off-black #111111 primary, cream canvas #faf9f6 surface, Fin Orange #ff5600 AI accent, report orange #fe4c02 data-viz, black-80 #313130 / black-60 #626260 / black-50 #7b7b78 neutrals, oat border #dedbd6, warm sand #d3cec6
  - **Background**: #faf9f6
- **Density**: precise — 4px button radius, 6px nav, 8px cards; 8px spacing base; breakpoints at 425/530/600/640/768/896px
- **Layout**: marketing editorial with billboard hero headlines, warm alternating sections, dashboard palette reserved for data-viz panels only
- **Imagery**: editorial photography and editorial illustration; multi-color report palette (blue #65b5ff, green #0bdf50, red #c41c1c, pink #ff2067, lime #b3e01c) for dashboards only
- **Motion**: scale(1.1) hover / scale(0.85) active on buttons — physical 'grow then press' transforms; oklab-based alpha borders for opacity management
