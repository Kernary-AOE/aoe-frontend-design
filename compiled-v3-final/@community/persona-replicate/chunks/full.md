# Replicate [persona] v1.0.0
Festival-energy AI platform: rb-freigeist-neue 700 at 128px manifesto scale on a white canvas with explosive orange #ea2804→magenta hero gradient, and 9999px pill radius applied absolutely to every element without exception.
domain: visual-design

## School
replicate

## Implies
- **Font**:
  - **Display**: rb-freigeist-neue (fallbacks: ui-sans-serif, system-ui) — weight 600–700, hero at 72px / -1.8px tracking, manifesto at 128px / 1.00 line-height
  - **Body**: basier-square (fallbacks: ui-sans-serif, system-ui) — geometric clean sans, weight 400
  - **Monospace**: jetbrains-mono (fallbacks: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco)
- **Color**:
  - **Temperature**: warm
  - **Palette**: brand red #ea2804 Replicate Red hero gradient start, secondary red #dd4425 button borders, dark #202020 text/surfaces, white #ffffff background, green #2b9a66 status, code-bg #24292e
  - **Background**: #ffffff
- **Density**: dramatic — 9999px pill radius on ALL elements without exception, 8px base spacing with 96/160/192px dramatic section breaks, fluid width no explicit breakpoints
- **Layout**: fluid full-width, explosive gradient hero, AI model pill-gallery grid, 128px manifesto closer section with images embedded between words
- **Imagery**: community-generated AI model output thumbnails in pill containers — user content is the marketing, no stock photography
- **Motion**: energetic — gradient animation on hero wash; no fine hover micro-interactions needed given pill-absolutism geometry
