# Superhuman [persona] v1.0.0
Luxury-productivity aesthetic: Super Sans VF at non-standard weight 540 / line-height 0.96 on pure white with a single cinematic hero in Mysteria Purple #1b1938, warm cream #e9e5dd CTAs, and lavender #cbb7fb as the sole accent.
domain: visual-design

## School
superhuman

## Implies
- **Font**:
  - **Display**: Super Sans VF variable font with fallbacks system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto
  - **Body**: Super Sans VF weight 460 — workhorse body weight between conventional 400 and 500
  - **Accent**: Messina Sans / Messina Serif / Messina Mono (Luzi Type) for product UI contexts
- **Color**:
  - **Temperature**: neutral
  - **Palette**: hero bg #1b1938 Mysteria Purple, primary text #292827 Charcoal Ink (warm near-black), dark text color(srgb 1 1 1 / 0.95), accent #cbb7fb Lavender Glow, link #714cb6 Amethyst Link, button bg #e9e5dd Warm Cream, border #dcd7d3 Parchment Border
  - **Background**: #ffffff
- **Density**: spacious — only two radius values (8px buttons/spans, 16px cards/links), 8px base spacing, 48–80px vertical section padding, ~1200px max-width
- **Layout**: cinematic single hero gradient dissolving to all-white content body, large product screenshots as content anchors, minimal surrounding copy
- **Imagery**: large product UI screenshots (email interface) as primary content — the interface sells itself; no stock photography
- **Motion**: cinematic but quiet — hero gradient is the one dramatic gesture; interactive states use opacity + underline, not color shifts or animation
