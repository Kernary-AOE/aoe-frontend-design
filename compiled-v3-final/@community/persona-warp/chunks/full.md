# Warp [persona] v1.0.0
Warm-dark developer lifestyle aesthetic: Matter Regular at weight 400 even on headlines, near-black canvas with earthy warm-gray undertones (not cold blue-black), #faf9f6 Warm Parchment text, and nature photography interleaved with terminal screenshots — the campfire dev tool.
domain: visual-design

## School
warp

## Implies
- **Font**:
  - **Display**: Matter Regular (Matter SQ Regular for select display) with fallbacks system sans
  - **Body**: Matter Regular weight 400 — same weight for headlines and body, hierarchy from size only
  - **Monospace**: Geist Mono (display mono) and Matter Mono Regular (body mono)
- **Color**:
  - **Temperature**: warm-neutral
  - **Palette**: primary text #faf9f6 Warm Parchment, button bg #353534 Earth Gray, borders/dark #454545 / #353534 Dark Charcoal, body reading #afaeac Ash Gray, secondary text #868584 Stone Gray, tertiary #666469 Muted Purple-Tint Gray, surface rgba(255,255,255,0.04) Frosted Veil, card border rgba(226,226,226,0.35) Mist Border
  - **Background**: near-black with earthy warm-gray undertones — no single hex, warm canvas not cold blue-black
- **Density**: cinematic — 8px base spacing, 80–120px section padding, ~1500px max-width (wide for photography), radius 4–6px small / 8px images+cards / 12–14px feature cards / 50px pill CTAs
- **Layout**: wide 1500px cinematic canvas, nature photography interleaved with terminal UI screenshots, editorial uppercase label categorization, sections feel like magazine spreads
- **Imagery**: golden-hour forests and landscapes interleaved with product terminal screenshots — unique lifestyle-dev visual language; no stock photography
- **Motion**: contemplative — no accent colors to animate; hover via ghostly border transitions rgba(226,226,226,0.35); no bounce, no urgency
