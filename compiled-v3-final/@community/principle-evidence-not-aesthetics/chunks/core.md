# EvidenceNotAesthetics [principle] v1.0.0
> Design decisions affecting user behavior, conversion, or accessibility must be grounded in evidence (user research, A/B data, analytics, heuristics) — not in aesthetic preference alone. Aesthetic preferences are valid inputs but they cannot override evidence of user harm or metric regression.
domain: frontend-design

## Implications
- An aesthetic objection ('I prefer this layout') must be paired with evidence or a testable hypothesis to carry weight in design review.
- Evidence from one context does not automatically transfer — test the evidence in the actual product context.
- Heuristics (Nielsen's 10) are evidence-level inputs. Personal opinion is not.
