# ProveNotPreventAiSlop [principle] v1.0.0
> The goal of anti-slop guidelines is to prove distinctive quality — not merely to prevent the worst defaults. A design that avoids all slop signals but makes no positive aesthetic statement has still failed. The bar is not 'does not look AI-generated' but 'looks unmistakably intentional.'
domain: frontend-design

## Implications
- Anti-slop checklists are necessary but not sufficient — pass them and then ask the brand distinctiveness question.
- A design with one genuinely bold, memorable decision is better than a design that merely avoids all 10 slop signals.
- The test is not the absence of slop signals but the presence of something worth remembering.
