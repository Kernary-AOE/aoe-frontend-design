# SubtleConceptualReference [principle] v1.0.0
> The subject of canvas art, poster design, or visual illustration should be woven as a quiet conceptual thread into form, color, and composition — not illustrated literally.
domain: frontend-design

## Attributed To
Anthropic Canvas Design Skill

## Applies To
- canvas-design
- poster-design
- hero visual composition
- product illustration

## Counter Examples
- Security app with a literal padlock illustration — safe but forgettable.
- Growth tool hero with literal rocket ship — cliché, zero conceptual depth.

## Examples
- A fintech app hero: instead of illustrating coins and growth charts, evoke the concept through ascending topology lines and cool-to-warm gradient shifts — the meaning is felt, not named.
- A writing tool poster: evoke 'stream of consciousness' through continuous flowing curves at different weights — not a pencil or a notebook.
- An analytics tool: use data-density grids as the compositional rhythm without showing a literal bar chart.

## Rationale
Literal depiction is obvious and forgettable. A refined, niche conceptual reference rewards viewers who recognize it while remaining compelling to those who don't — like a jazz musician quoting another song. Only those who know will catch it, but everyone appreciates the music.
