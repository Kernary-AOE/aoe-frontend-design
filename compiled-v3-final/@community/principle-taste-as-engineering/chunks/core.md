# TasteAsEngineering [principle] v1.0.0
> Aesthetic taste in design is an engineering discipline: it is learnable, reproducible, testable, and decomposable into specific decisions. 'Good taste' is not a mystical quality — it is the sum of many small, specific, evidence-informed design decisions, each of which can be named, documented, and enforced in a knowledge system.
domain: frontend-design

## Implications
- Every aesthetic choice should be nameable and documentable as an atom.
- If you cannot explain why a design decision is correct in concrete terms, it is a hunch — test it before shipping.
- The opposite of good taste is not bad taste — it is unexamined taste. Examining any taste systematically is the engineering discipline.
