# AlignChangesToFindings [rule] v1.0.0
> Proposed design changes must be cross-referenced against documented pain points, validated patterns, and known success metrics before finalizing direction. Any conflict between the proposal and existing research must be surfaced explicitly.
domain: frontend-design

## Severity
warning

## Applies When
Project has research documents such as usability study findings, experiment outcomes, or analytics reports.

## Verify By
Cross-reference the proposed change against documented pain points and validated patterns. Note any conflicts in the design rationale. A proposal that ignores available research must be sent back.

## Rationale
Ignoring prior research wastes its investment and reintroduces solved problems. A design change that contradicts a validated finding is riskier than one that extends it — that risk must be named and accepted deliberately.
