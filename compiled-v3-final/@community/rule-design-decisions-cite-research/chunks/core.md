# DesignDecisionsCiteResearch [rule] v1.0.0
> When usability studies, interviews, surveys, or analytics exist, every design proposal must explicitly reference the supporting evidence — naming at least one data source (test, interview finding, funnel metric) in its rationale.
domain: frontend-design

## Severity
warning

## Applies When
Proposing or reviewing any design change when research artifacts or analytics are available.

## Verify By
Check that the design rationale names at least one data source. A proposal with no evidence citation when evidence exists must be sent back for research review.
