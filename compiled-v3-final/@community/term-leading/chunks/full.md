# Leading [term] v1.0.0
> The vertical distance between successive baselines of text — historically the height of the lead strips inserted between lines of metal type. In CSS, leading is controlled by the `line-height` property and is the single most important typographic variable for reading comfort and visual rhythm.
domain: visual-design

## Meaning
The vertical distance between successive baselines of text — historically the height of the lead strips inserted between lines of metal type. In CSS, leading is controlled by the `line-height` property and is the single most important typographic variable for reading comfort and visual rhythm.

## Aliases
- line-height
- linespacing
- interlinear spacing
