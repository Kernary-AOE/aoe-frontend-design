# InterPurpleDark [counter-example] v1.0.0
A statistically over-represented combination in AI-generated frontends: Inter as the typeface, a saturated purple (#7c3aed or similar) as the brand accent, on a near-black (#0f0f0f or #111827) background. The pattern appears in the majority of LLM-generated dashboards, landing pages, and SaaS UIs because these values appear frequently in training data (Tailwind defaults, shadcn/ui defaults, Vercel-adjacent starters).
