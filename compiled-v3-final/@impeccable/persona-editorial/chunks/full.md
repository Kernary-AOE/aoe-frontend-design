# Editorial [persona] v1.0.0
An aesthetic persona derived from high-quality editorial publishing: newspapers, literary magazines, annual reports. Characterized by deliberate whitespace, serif display type, restrained color use, and strong typographic hierarchy.
domain: visual-design

## School
editorial

## Implies
- **Font**:
  - **Display**: serif — e.g. Freight Display, Canela, Playfair Display, GT Super
  - **Body**: humanist-sans or transitional-serif — e.g. Söhne, Source Serif, Freight Text
- **Color**:
  - **Temperature**: neutral-warm
  - **Palette**: limited — 2 dominant, 1 accent, generous neutrals
  - **Background**: off-white (#f9f7f4) or warm white, not pure #ffffff
- **Density**: generous — vertical rhythm 1.6–1.8, large margins, breathing room
- **Layout**: asymmetric grid with wide margins, text columns 55–65ch
- **Imagery**: high-quality photography or editorial illustration, not stock icons
- **Motion**: minimal — reveal on scroll, no decorative looping animations
