# SwissModernist [persona] v1.0.0
Müller-Brockmann on the web: mathematical baseline grids, neo-grotesque display set tight, ruled horizontal lines, asymmetric typographic compositions, one accent color in a sea of neutral. Form follows function follows grid.
domain: visual-design

## School
swiss-modernist

## Implies
- **Font**:
  - **Display**: neo-grotesque, large and tight-tracked — e.g. Helvetica Now, Neue Haas Grotesk, Akzidenz-Grotesk, ABC Diatype, GT America, Söhne
  - **Body**: same family as display, smaller weight contrast — e.g. Helvetica Now Text, Neue Haas Grotesk Text, ABC Diatype, Inter Display
- **Color**:
  - **Temperature**: cool-neutral
  - **Palette**: monochrome plus one chromatic accent — typically pure red (#e30613 Helvetica-poster red) or international-blue, no gradients
  - **Background**: #ffffff with hairline #e5e5e5 rules, or inverted #0a0a0a with #ffffff text
- **Density**: measured — vertical rhythm 1.5 locked to 8px baseline grid, generous outer margins, tight inner letter spacing (-0.02em on display)
- **Layout**: 12-column or 16-column grid, asymmetric placement, hairline rule-lines (1px solid) demarcating sections, ample top/bottom space
- **Imagery**: high-contrast B&W photography, geometric shapes (circle/square/rectangle as content), no decorative illustration
- **Motion**: minimal — instant or 120ms linear, no spring physics, no parallax

- **Typography Required**:

  - **Background**: #ffffff with #e5e5e5 hairline rules
  - **Palette**: monochrome + ONE accent (no gradients)
  - **Accent**: #e30613 Helvetica-poster red or international-blue
