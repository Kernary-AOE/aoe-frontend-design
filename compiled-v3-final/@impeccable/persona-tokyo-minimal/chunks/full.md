# TokyoMinimal [persona] v1.0.0
Japanese minimalism translated to web: extreme negative space (ma), hairline rules, soft greys on warm off-white, mixed-script typography (Noto Sans JP + Inter), tatami-grid spatial logic. The page breathes like a tea ceremony — every element earned its place.
domain: visual-design

## School
tokyo-minimal

## Implies
- **Font**:
  - **Display**: humanist-sans paired with Japanese sans — e.g. Noto Sans JP, Hiragino Sans, Yu Gothic, Inter, ABC Diatype, Söhne
  - **Body**: Noto Sans JP, Hiragino Kaku Gothic ProN, Yu Gothic, system-ui — paired with Inter or Söhne for Latin
  - **Accent**: Sazanami Mincho or Noto Serif JP for vertical/numbered ornaments only
- **Color**:
  - **Temperature**: neutral-warm
  - **Palette**: warm off-white + 3 soft greys + ink-black, no chromatic accent — #fafaf7 / #ededea / #b8b6b0 / #4a4845 / #1a1a1a
  - **Background**: #fafaf7 (warm paper) — never #ffffff, never tinted blue
- **Density**: generous 1.7 — line-height 1.8 for Japanese, 1.6 for Latin, padding 32/48/64, paragraph spacing > line-height
- **Layout**: single-column with extreme outer margins (15-25% viewport), max content width 540-620px, hairline rules (#ededea 1px) as section dividers, vertical numerical ornaments
- **Imagery**: Hiroshi Sugimoto-style long-exposure B&W photography, soft-focus product shots on natural backgrounds, no isometric illustrations, no emoji
- **Motion**: minimal — 240ms ease-out fade-in only, no parallax, scroll progress indicators acceptable, hover states extremely subtle (1% opacity shift)
