# WhyNotSerif [provocation] v1.0.0
domain: visual-design

## Label
Why Not Serif?

## Trigger
agent about to select a sans-serif typeface as the primary display font

## Question
Why are you using a sans-serif for display headings? Have you considered that a high-quality serif (Canela, GT Super, Freight Display) would immediately distinguish this design from the ~80% of AI-generated interfaces that default to sans-serif? What specific visual goal does sans-serif serve better here — and is that goal actually stated in the brief?

## Intent
Challenge the AI-slop default of universal sans-serif usage. Serif type at display scale is not 'old fashioned' — it signals deliberate editorial craft and immediately separates the work from commodity output. The question is not 'should we use serif' but 'have we consciously decided not to, and why'.

## Resolution Paths
-
  - **Context**: Dense product UI, data-heavy (@impeccable/persona-dense-pragmatist)
  - **Answer**: Sans-serif is correct — legibility at small sizes and visual alignment with data tables justifies it.
-
  - **Context**: Editorial, marketing, brand landing page
  - **Answer**: Strong case for serif — proceed with @impeccable/persona-editorial guidance.
-
  - **Context**: Ambiguous SaaS product
  - **Answer**: Explore both: prototype serif display with sans body vs. geometric sans throughout. Let contrast testing decide.
