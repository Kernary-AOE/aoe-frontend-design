# CasualWarm [voice] v1.0.0
The voice used by friendly consumer brands. Second person, contractions welcome, occasional emoji for tone (never for decoration). Sounds like Mailchimp, Duolingo, Discord, Notion's onboarding.
domain: consumer-brand

## Label
Casual Warm

## Tone
friendly + close-talking + a little playful

## Emphasis
you-first → small specific delight → low-stakes invitation

## Emotional Arc
curious → smiling → comfortable enough to click

## Patterns
-
  - **Label**: Direct address opener
  - **Template**: Hey — {observation about the user's state}. {Next step in plain words}.
  - **Example**: Hey — looks like your inbox has been busy. Want a hand sorting it?
-
  - **Label**: Contractions + short clause
  - **Template**: {Subject} {contraction} {object}. {Reassurance}.
  - **Example**: You've got this. We'll save your draft as you go.
-
  - **Label**: Playful aside (in dashes)
  - **Template**: {Statement} — {gentle joke or aside} — {continuation}.
  - **Example**: Your streak is at 47 days — yes, we're impressed — keep going.
-
  - **Label**: Friendly micro-CTA
  - **Template**: {Invitation verb} {object} when you're ready.
  - **Example**: Pick a theme when you're ready.
-
  - **Label**: Empty-state encouragement
  - **Template**: Nothing here yet. {Concrete first action} to get started.
  - **Example**: Nothing here yet. Add your first project to get started.
-
  - **Label**: Tone-emoji (never decoration)
  - **Template**: {Sentence ending in a feeling}. {Optional one emoji that doubles the feeling}
  - **Example**: Your settings are saved. ✓

## Prohibitions
- Do not stack emoji (one per message, max — and only when it carries meaning).
- Do not use exclamation marks more than once per screen.
- Do not use slang that ages quickly ('on fleek', 'lit', 'no cap').
- Do not write 'sorry' for routine system events — reserve apology for real failures.
- Do not infantilize the user with 'oopsie' or 'whoopsie'.
- Do not write paragraphs longer than three short sentences.
