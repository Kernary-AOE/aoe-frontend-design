# MarketingBold [voice] v1.0.0
The voice used on confident product landing pages and feature launches. Verb-first, short, declarative, kinetic. Sounds like Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Framer headlines — momentum over explanation.
domain: marketing-landing

## Label
Marketing Bold

## Tone
confident + kinetic

## Emphasis
verb → outcome → proof — no hedging, no apology, no qualifier

## Emotional Arc
intrigue → conviction → urge to click — reader feels the product is already shipping without them

## Patterns
-
  - **Label**: Headline (verb-first imperative)
  - **Template**: {Verb} {object}. {Verb} {outcome}.
  - **Example**: Ship faster. Move on.
-
  - **Label**: Subhead (concrete proof)
  - **Template**: {Number}× {improvement}. {Concrete-feature-1}. {Concrete-feature-2}.
  - **Example**: 2× faster page loads. Instant search. Zero config.
-
  - **Label**: Tagline (3-word manifesto)
  - **Template**: {Verb} the {abstract-noun}.
  - **Example**: Build the future. Ship the web. Design the next.
-
  - **Label**: CTA (action + time)
  - **Template**: {Verb} in {duration} →
  - **Example**: Start in 30 seconds  →
-
  - **Label**: Section opener (anti-status-quo)
  - **Template**: Stop {old-behavior}. Start {new-behavior}.
  - **Example**: Stop fighting your stack. Start building.
-
  - **Label**: Social proof (named, specific)
  - **Template**: {Number} teams at {recognized-brands} {verb} on {product}.
  - **Example**: 40,000 teams at Vercel, Notion, and Ramp ship on Linear.

## Prohibitions
- Do not write 'we are excited to announce' — it telegraphs corporate distance.
- Do not use 'simply', 'just', or 'easily' — they erode the confidence the voice depends on.
- Do not write sentences over 12 words for headlines.
- Do not use the passive voice ('is enabled by') in primary copy.
- Do not stack three adjectives — pick the strongest one.
- Do not use exclamation marks — confidence does not shout.
